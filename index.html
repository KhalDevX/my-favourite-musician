MY FAVOURITE MUSICIAN

Ariana Grande

Ariana Grande is an American singer, songwriter and actress known for her powerful vocals, popular songs and acting career. This website explores her life, music and achievements.

Learn About Ariana

Biography

  • Full name: Ariana Grande-Butera
  • Date of birth: June 26, 1993
  • Place of birth: Boca Raton, Florida, USA
  • Nationality: American
  • Musical genres: Pop, R&B and dance-pop

Ariana Grande developed an interest in music and acting when she was young. She performed in theatre productions before beginning her professional career.

In 2008, she appeared on Broadway in the musical 13. She later became famous for playing Cat Valentine in Nickelodeon's Victorious and Sam & Cat.

Her early experience in theatre and television helped her become a confident performer. She later focused on music and became known for her wide vocal range and pop and R&B sound.

Musical History and Career Journey

2008-2012: Early Career

Ariana began professionally in musical theatre and gained attention through her television role as Cat Valentine. She continued developing her singing.

2013-2015: Music Breakthrough

She released her first album, Yours Truly, in 2013. The song "The Way," featuring Mac Miller, helped introduce her to music audiences. Her next album, My Everything, included "Problem" and "Break Free."

2016-2019: Worldwide Success

Dangerous Woman, Sweetener and thank u, next showed the development of her sound. Songs such as "Into You," "no tears left to cry" and "7 rings" helped strengthen her popularity.

2020-Present: Music and Acting

Ariana released Positions in 2020 and eternal sunshine in 2024. She also played Glinda in the film Wicked.

Major Collaborations

Ariana has worked with Nicki Minaj on "Side to Side," The Weeknd on "Love Me Harder," Lady Gaga on "Rain On Me," and Cynthia Erivo on "Defying Gravity."

Awards and Achievements

  • Grammy Award won: Best Pop Vocal Album for Sweetener (2019).
  • Grammy Award won: Best Pop Duo/Group Performance for "Rain On Me" with Lady Gaga (2021).
  • Billboard achievement: "thank u, next" reached number one on the US Billboard Hot 100.
  • Billboard achievement: "7 rings" also reached number one on the US Billboard Hot 100.
  • Album achievement: Yours Truly debuted at number one on the US Billboard 200.
  • Academy Award nomination: Nominated for Best Supporting Actress for Wicked at the 2025 ceremony.
  • Golden Globe nomination: Nominated for her role in Wicked at the 2025 ceremony.
